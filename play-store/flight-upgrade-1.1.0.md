## Gameplay

- Five worlds, 15 stages per world; all existing valid star records are retained.
- Deterministic encounter sequences, three lane patterns, recovery sections, regular fuel stops.
- First two stages have no blocking hazards. Later worlds introduce drones and lasers gradually.
- World completion unlocks the next world; earlier 30-star unlocks remain valid.
- Three independent star criteria: finish, collect crystals, complete the objective with at most one hit.
- Collected crystals and bonus wallet rewards are counted separately. First-clear bonuses cannot be claimed again.
- Bounded cruise speed and height-scaled controls, hard ceiling/floor collision limits on rotation too.
- Pause freezes the simulation and power-up timers. Backgrounding pauses; returning requires explicit resume.
- Stage briefing, exit beacon, per-star results, compact landscape results and persistent settings navigation.

## Audio And Art

- New original 92 BPM / 16-bar music loop, generated reproducibly with tools/master-flight-audio.py.
- Filtered original engine/impact recordings, silent coasting, smooth thrust envelope and event rate limits.
- Independent effects/music/engine sliders. Warning and impact ducking.
- Native Android haptics, native sample preload, content-addressed audio cache.
- Background/pause stops short effects as well as loops. Zero volume is respected.
- Android no longer decodes a duplicate Web Audio sound bank; temporary audition assets are excluded from the bundle.
- Two new imagegen artworks: deep-space-v2.png and volcanic-cavern-v2.png in app/wwwroot/game/assets/generated.
- Existing ice, emerald and storm art retained, with additional cached scenery layers.
